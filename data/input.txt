MiniGPT is a small language model built for learning.
A language model predicts the next token from the tokens that came before it.
A token can be a character, a word, or a subword unit.
The transformer uses attention to combine information from earlier positions.
Causal attention prevents a position from looking at future tokens.
Training adjusts model parameters to reduce the next token prediction loss.
The model begins with random parameters and learns patterns from examples.
A small model trained on a tiny dataset has limited ability.
Good experiments use clean data, repeatable settings, and careful evaluation.
